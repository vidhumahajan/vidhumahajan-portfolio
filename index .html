How I work

Three habits, learnt from live events and doctor calls.

Start with a conversation

Before assuming what a user wants, I get them on a call. With doctors that meant asking how they actually spend the ninety seconds between patients. Reports tell you the size of a problem. People tell you the shape of it.

Read the room while it happens

Hosting a live show teaches you to spot confusion before anyone says a word. The same signals show up in a funnel: people hesitate, drop off, fill half a form. I change the flow first and the copy second.

Write down what actually happened

After campus outreach I reported response and footfall college by college, so the next round targeted the places that converted. Every push should leave behind a number the team can act on.
